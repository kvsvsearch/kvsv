Contribute to KVSV

KVSV's universal intake process for family research, corrections, professional submissions, oral history, and community archive contributions.

Submission creates a case. Submission does not create a public record.

Everything you send is reviewed by KVSV researchers before anything is published.

How this works

  1. You submit the Contribute form.
  2. An automatic case record is created.
  3. Consent and completeness gate.
  4. Case type and priority assigned.
  5. Research tier assigned.
  6. Research and evidence workflow.
  7. Quality review by a second researcher.
  8. Final disposition.
  • Submissions are reviewed.
  • Nothing is published automatically.
  • KVSV may contact the submitter.
  • Public-search publication requires approval.
  • Unapproved information remains private.
  • The same evidence standards apply to every submitter.

Required gates

Gate 0 - Intake integrity
  • Submission complete enough to evaluate
  • Submitter contactable
  • Consent language accepted
  • Spam / malicious material flagged
  • Living-person and sensitive-information concerns flagged
Gate 1 - Case acceptance
  • Relevant to KVSV
  • Duplicate check performed
  • Enough information exists to begin
  • Research tier assigned
  • Clarification requested if necessary
Gate 2 - Evidence and identity
  • Names and alternate spellings evaluated
  • Dates and locations evaluated
  • Relationships evaluated
  • Source quality evaluated
  • Contradictions recorded
  • Possible identity conflation flagged
  • Submitted claims separated from documented facts
Gate 3 - Comprehensive analysis
  • Build working family structure
  • Search relevant records and repositories
  • Compare independent sources
  • Track aliases and variant spellings
  • Record positive and negative findings
  • Identify unresolved questions
  • Record confidence levels
  • Preserve citations and evidence links
  • Maintain a research log
Gate 4 - Internal quality review
  • Second reviewer checks the evidence
  • Relationships and dates challenged
  • Unsupported claims removed or labeled
  • Privacy and consent reviewed
  • Duplicate records checked
  • Final case classification approved
Gate 5 - Final disposition
  • Approved for public search
  • Approved with limited fields
  • Returned for clarification
  • Held for further research
  • Restricted as private
  • Rejected
