Professional Genealogist Consultation & Expert Review

A second, expert look before you trust the tree.

Sometimes the question isn't where to start research — it's whether research already done, by you or someone else, actually holds up. KVSV offers an independent expert review of existing genealogy work: checking citations, testing conclusions against the underlying records, and giving you a clear, honest verdict on what's solid and what needs more work.

Illustrative Example: From Question to Next Step

A customer inherited a relative's decades-old research binder and wants to know if it can be trusted before building on it. The next step is a structured review, not a skim: each generation's key claims are traced back to their cited sources — or lack of one — and rated. Four generations hold up well; a fifth turns out to rest on a family story with no record ever actually checked, which is exactly the kind of thing worth knowing before it goes any further.

Illustrative example only — generic and fictional, not a real case, client, or testimonial.

Who This Service Is For

Anyone with an existing tree, report, or research file who wants an independent check before relying on it; people who inherited someone else's research and don't know how trustworthy it is; researchers preparing work for a lineage society, legal matter, or publication who need a second set of eyes.

What KVSV Helps You Do

  • Review an existing tree, report, or research file for citation quality and evidentiary strength.
  • Re-check key conclusions against the original records cited.
  • Identify unsupported assumptions or hint-based leaps presented as fact.
  • Rate findings by confidence level, matching the standard used across KVSV's own research.
  • Recommend specific next steps to close the gaps the review finds.

What This Service Helps You Do and The Value You Receive

This service helps you find out, honestly, how solid your existing research really is before you build further on it or submit it somewhere that matters. The value you receive is a plain-spoken expert verdict — what's well-supported, what's shaky, and exactly what would need to change to make a weak conclusion strong.

What You Receive

  • A written review of the submitted tree, report, or file.
  • A confidence rating on each major conclusion reviewed.
  • A list of unsupported assumptions or citation gaps found.
  • Specific, prioritized recommendations for strengthening the weak points.

Process

  1. Submit and scope — you share what you want reviewed and what it's for — personal confidence, a lineage society, a legal matter.
  2. Independent review — conclusions are re-checked against the underlying records, not just re-read.
  3. Honest report — a written verdict is delivered with confidence ratings and concrete next steps.

Human Review, Privacy, and Limitations

This is an independent research review, not a certification, legal opinion, or guarantee of acceptance by any third party (lineage society, court, or institution). Confidence ratings reflect the evidence reviewed at the time of review and may change as new records surface.

Proprietary boundary: Modeled after the research discipline of the KVSV platform and research portal, without disclosing or reproducing proprietary KVSV systems, internal workbooks, databases, backend systems, communications, or daily operating methods.

FAQ

Will you tell me my research is wrong even if I don't want to hear it?

Yes — an honest review only has value if it's honest; we report what the evidence supports, not what would be more comfortable to hear.

Can this support a lineage society application?

We can review against a named society's stated evidentiary standard and flag gaps, though formal submission requirements are always the society's own to confirm.

What if my research turns out mostly right?

Then that's exactly what gets reported — a review that confirms strong work is just as useful as one that finds problems.
