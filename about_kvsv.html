About this research

What you're looking at, and how it was built

KVSV is a genealogy research portal and community project centered on lineages and mix-heritages that are often overlooked by mainstream genealogists and institutions — and the branches connected them. The public search portal draws from a vast array of access points, from the U.S. Census to the National Archives, WikiTree, FamilySearch, and more. The records here are public records and are not subject to the same rigorous tier-level standards or protocols as the research arm or commissioned project studies, and should be taken at face value. Every entry in the KVSV databases is built from primary records and source materials, including private and public indexes. KVSV strives to be a "source-first" portal, not a commercial ancestry database, and offers customizable genealogical research frameworks for those wanting to establish canonical trees and lineage mapping. For more on customization, please contact us for details.

How this research is done

Primary sources come first. No fact is stated without a source behind it. When two sources disagree, the disagreement is logged, not quietly resolved. Nothing is merged into the record without review.

What's public vs. private

Public Search shows only the fields cleared for public display. Full family research, restricted notes, and information about living people stay in the private portals — not here.

Terms

Entity
One person tracked in this database.
Narrative
The written account of what's known about them, in plain language.
Citation
The actual source behind a fact.
Source Tier
How strong that source is — see above.

Privacy

No living person's sensitive information is published according to standards. Restricted genealogy research — the kind that touches people who are still alive — stays behind the private portal, not on the public search portal. All private or customized search portals, outside of the researcher's own portal, are password protected and secured in the Cloud.
