About this research

What you're looking at, and how it was built

KVSV is a genealogy research project centered on Coosa County, Alabama — the families connected to it, and the Creek Nation history that shaped this part of the Southeast. Every entry here is built from primary records and family testimony, not assembled from someone else's tree. This is source-first research, not a commercial ancestry database.

How to read a Tier

Every person in this database carries a Tier — a plain measure of how strong the evidence behind them is. Here's what each one means:

  1. Primary record. An actual document — a census entry, a deed, a treaty, a will.
  2. Strong secondary source. Independently corroborated by more than one record or account.
  3. Published transcription or secondary source. Not yet cross-checked against the original.
  4. A lead. Often a family tree or a screenshot, logged because it might matter later — not yet verified.

How this research is done

Primary sources come first. No fact is stated without a source behind it. When two sources disagree, the disagreement is logged, not quietly resolved. Nothing is merged into the record without review.

What's public vs. private

Public Search shows only the fields cleared for public display. Full family research, restricted notes, and information about living people stay in the private portals — not here.

Terms

Privacy

No living person's sensitive information is published here without their consent. Restricted genealogy research — the kind that touches people who are still alive — stays behind the private portal, not on this page.
