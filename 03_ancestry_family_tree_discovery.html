Ancestry Research & Family Tree Discovery

Build a tree you can stand behind.

Online hints can grow a family tree fast — and grow it wrong just as fast. KVSV’s tree-building approach adds each person only when a record supports the connection. You work with a family tree where every relationship is sourced, uncertain links are visibly marked, and the difference between documented fact and family tradition is always clear.

Illustrative Example: From Question to Next Step

A customer’s tree shows a possible second marriage for a great-grandfather, added by a website hint. The next step is verification: pull both candidate marriage records, compare names, witnesses, and locations, then either cite the correct one or mark the link unverified. The customer ends with one corrected relationship and a citation attached — or a clearly labeled open question.

Illustrative example only — generic and fictional, not a real case, client, or testimonial.

Who This Service Is For

Customers who want a verified family tree structure; people with trees full of unsourced hints who want them checked; families preparing a documented tree for a reunion, book, or professional handoff.

What KVSV Helps You Do

  • Audit an existing tree and flag unsourced or conflicting connections.
  • Add new generations only with cited record support.
  • Mark confidence levels on every relationship link.
  • Separate documented lines from tradition-based lines visually and in notes.
  • Prepare a sourced tree export and summary for your own records.

What This Service Helps You Do and The Value You Receive

This service helps you turn a hint-built tree into a source-supported family structure — or build one that way from the start. The value you receive is a tree where every connection carries its evidence, so you can share it confidently, knowing exactly which branches are solid and which still need work.

What You Receive

  • A sourced family tree with confidence markers on each relationship.
  • A list of unsourced or conflicting connections that need verification.
  • Citation notes tied to each person and relationship.
  • A plain-language summary of the tree’s strengths and open questions.

Process

  1. Tree audit or fresh frame — we review what exists or set up a clean structure.
  2. Source-driven building — each new connection is added only with record support and a citation.
  3. Confidence marking and handoff — links are graded, open questions listed, and the tree is delivered with its documentation.

Work Examples

Illustrative and generic — not testimonials or real client cases.

  • An audit of a 120-person online tree finds 14 unsourced parent-child links; each is flagged, and seven are confirmed or corrected using vital records.
  • A new tree branch is added only after two independent records support the connection; the link is marked high-confidence with both citations attached.

Human Review, Privacy, and Limitations

Tree work documents what records support; it does not determine legal parentage, prove biological relationships, authenticate documents, or replace DNA or legal review. Tradition-based lines are preserved and labeled — never presented as documented fact.

Proprietary boundary: Modeled after the research discipline of the KVSV platform and research portal, without disclosing or reproducing proprietary KVSV systems, internal workbooks, databases, backend systems, communications, or daily operating methods.

FAQ

Should I delete my unsourced tree?

No. We audit and flag it; many hints verify, and the ones that don’t become a documented to-do list.

How do you mark uncertain links?

Each relationship carries a confidence label and a note explaining the supporting — or missing — evidence.

Can DNA results be added to the tree?

DNA matches can inform research leads, but DNA conclusions require qualified specialist review and are never asserted from hints alone.
