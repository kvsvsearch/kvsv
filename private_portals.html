Private Family Portal Prototype Preview

Your family's own research portal

A private, sourced, tiered research space built the same way this project builds its own - sourced, tiered, and never silently overwritten.

This is a demo. Everything you see here is placeholder content. No real family records are stored, no authentication is required, and nothing is saved anywhere. This page is a layout preview only.

What this portal will include

People

Your family list, with sources and evidence tiers attached to every name.

Family Tree

A horizontal tree view, built from your own verified records.

Documents & Media

A place for scanned records, photos, and family media.

Oral History

Recorded family testimony, attached directly to the record.
